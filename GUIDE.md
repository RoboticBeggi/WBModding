# How to play Teyvat Chronicle

This guide covers everything: what to click, what happens, and how the pieces fit together.
The same guide is in the game under **Teyvat tab → How to Play** (or **Teyvat Archive → Guide**).

---

## Quick start (5 minutes)

1. **Install:** NeoModLoader from the Steam Workshop, then this mod in `<WorldBox>/Mods/TeyvatChronicle`
   (see the README).
2. **Make a world:** any size. Let it run until a few kingdoms exist (5–10 in-game years).
3. **Open the Teyvat tab** (the book icon in the god-powers bar), then **Teyvat Archive**.
4. **Chronicle tab:** pick a pace (Normal is a good first run), then press **Begin**.
5. **Watch:** the Old World starts with seven element-coloured Dragon Sovereigns and a cutscene.
6. **Click any unit** you see glowing. The **Resonance panel** opens beside its window.

That's it. Everything else is optional depth.

---

## The flow of a playthrough

```
World grows ─► Begin the Chronicle ─► Eras unfold (cutscenes, effects on the world)
                                         │
             ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
             ▼                                                        ▼
   Powers appear on units                                Ordinary people train & fight
   (Sovereigns, Shades, Archons,                          ─► potential ─► a Vision awakens
    Harbingers, Visions...)                                           │
             │                                                        ▼
             └──────────► Battles, moves, duels, reactions ◄── Vision bearers drill, spar,
                                     │                          train at dummies
                                     ▼
                     XP ─► Ranks ─► Breakthrough Trials ─► Ascension stars
                                     │                ─► Talents, Constellations
                                     ▼
                     Exaltation (rank 70, ★5, C4) ─► Godhood (claim an empty Archon throne)
```

The Chronicle supplies the **history**: who the gods are and when the great events happen. The
Resonance system supplies the **heroes**: ordinary people rising through training and battle. The two
meet in battle. You can play with either one alone. Turn eras off, never press Begin and just raise
heroes, or let the Chronicle run and only watch.

## 1. The Teyvat tab

**First row: the Chronicle**

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Teyvat Archive | Opens the main window (Chronicle, Legends, Powers, Guide) |
| Begin | Starts the Chronicle from the era chosen in Settings |
| Timeline | Opens the Archive's Chronicle tab |
| Advance | Makes the next event happen now |
| Pause / Resume | Holds the Chronicle (the world keeps running) |
| Status | Shows the next event and its countdown |
| Stop | Ends the Chronicle on this world |

**Second row: powers.** For most of these, select the power, then click a unit:

| Power | What it does |
|---|---|
| Legends | Opens the Legends ranking |
| How to Play | Opens this guide in the game |
| Toggle HUD | Shows or hides the top-right panel |
| Inspect | Opens the unit with its Resonance panel |
| Grant Vision | Awakens a Vision; on a Vision bearer, +5 ranks |
| Shift Element | Cycles a Vision bearer's element |
| Grant Delusion | Gives a Fatui Delusion: strong, but it costs lifespan |
| Ascend | +10 ranks (awakens a Vision first if needed) |
| Bestow Constellation | +1 Constellation |
| Crown Archon | Makes the unit the Archon of its element |
| Training Ground | Click a place to mark it: units train ×2 there. Click again to remove it |

You can also give any power through WorldBox's own **trait editor**: open a unit and look for the
**Teyvat: Elements / Exalted / Divine / Primordial / Curses** groups.

## 2. The Teyvat Archive

A draggable window (drag its title bar) with four tabs.

**Chronicle**
- **Status card:** the current era, the next event and its countdown, and a progress bar across all 45 events.
- **Pacing:** Lore (~910 years), Normal (~470), Fast (~160), or Custom (Normal × the time-scale setting).
  Your choice is saved immediately.
- **Begin, Advance, Pause, Stop, Settings.**
- **Event list:** every event grouped by era. A green `+` means done, a gold `>` means next, `-` means upcoming.
  ★ marks major events. **Click an event** to read its story, then **Start here** to begin the
  Chronicle from that exact point.

**Legends:** the 40 mightiest powers, ranked by tier, then rank, then Constellation. Click one to jump to them and open their window.

**Powers:** the tier codex (what each tier is and how to get it) and the elemental reactions table.

**Guide:** this guide.

**The HUD** (top right) shows the next Chronicle event with its countdown and the top 5 legends. Click
a name to jump to them. Drag it by its title, or hide it with Toggle HUD.

## 3. The Resonance panel

It opens automatically beside WorldBox's unit window whenever you click a unit, and updates twice a second.

- **Identity:** element emblem, name, power, and the tier badge in that tier's colour.
- **Rank:** Rank 1–90 with an XP bar in the element's colour, and the six **Ascension stars**.
- **Breakthrough:** if the unit is stuck at a Breakthrough, the Trial it needs and its progress (for example "2/3 sessions").
- **Talents:** Strike, Skill and Burst bars (up to Lv 13 with Constellations).
- **Constellation:** six star nodes that light up in the element colour.
- **Now:** what the unit is doing. For example: Training at a dummy (mentor ×1.5, Training Ground ×2),
  Drilling, Sparring, In battle, Locked in a duel, or Wandering.
- **Record:** foes, elites, duels won, spars won, training sessions.
- **Moves:** every move with its category (STRIKE / SKILL / BURST) and live state: ready, seconds left
  to recharge, or energy (for example 40/100). `*` marks a move with a cutscene, `+` a move with an impact frame.
- **Buttons:** Grant Vision, Ascend +10, + Constellation, Follow (camera follows the unit), Legends, Archive.

For an ordinary person, the panel shows their **potential** toward a Vision.

## 4. The Chronicle: Teyvat's history on your world

The Chronicle has 45 events in 11 eras:
- The Old World
- When the Doves Held Branches
- The Unified Civilization
- The War of Funerary Flame
- The Archon War
- The Age of the Seven
- The Cataclysm
- After the Cataclysm
- The Present Day
- Song of the Welkin Moon (Nod-Krai)
- The Snezhnaya Crisis

**Major events** play as a **cutscene**: letterbox bars, the camera flies to the place, time slows, a
spotlight falls, a title card appears, and a sound plays. Other events show a coloured banner. Each
event is also written to the world history. Countdown warnings appear 10 years and 1 year before major events.

**What events do to the world:**
- They spawn the seven **Dragon Sovereigns**, each in its own element design.
- They give the **Heavenly Principles and the Four Shades** to the strongest beings.
- They shatter the moons, drop **Celestial Nails** (meteors) on cities, and found **Khaenri'ah**
  (one kingdom is renamed) and later destroy it.
- They crown **the Seven**, let the Fatui gather the Gnoses one by one, and awaken **the Traveler**.
- They give **Columbina** the Trilunar Authority and unleash **Ronova's Curse of Death**.

**Starting later:** with **catch-up** on (the default), starting at any event first replays the
set-up effects of everything before it, silently. So the Archons already reign if you start in the
present day. Destruction is never replayed.

**Customise everything:** turn eras or minor events on and off, disable destruction or wars, change
the pacing, or rewrite the whole history in `data/chronicle.custom.json` (see CHRONICLE.md).

## 5. Powers and tiers

| Tier | Who | Aura you'll see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Vision Bearer | Visions, Delusions, Allogenes | A faint element shimmer |
| 2 Exalted | Adepti, Harbingers, Witches, Abyss Heralds, Demon Gods | Element ki flames |
| 3 Archon | The Seven (Gnosis holders) | Flames under a golden halo |
| 4 Dragon Sovereign | Seven element dragons, each with a custom design | Element motes and flames |
| 5 Shade | Life, Death, Time, Space | Crimson flames and dark motes |
| 6 Lunar | Moon Goddesses, Trilunar, Nibelung | Three orbiting moons |
| 7 Heavenly Principles | The Primordial One | Turning rays of light |

A unit uses the moveset of its **highest** tier. Each tier has 5–7 moves, and higher tiers hit far harder.

## 6. How heroes grow (Resonance)

**Ordinary people**
- They build **potential** by training in their city, at training dummies, and by fighting.
- At **100 potential**, each year there's a chance a Vision awakens. Kings are twice as likely; traits
  like *ambitious*, *chosen one* and *lucky* raise the odds; and a desperate battle (under 25% health)
  makes it six times as likely.
- Awakening plays a ceremony: the world stops, rings close in, then a pillar of light.

**Vision bearers gain XP from:**

| Source | XP |
|---|---|
| Drilling in their city | 400 / year |
| Training at a dummy | 800 / year |
| Sparring with another bearer | 35–45 per bout |
| Using moves, kills | + |
| Duels | win +80, loss +30 |

Multipliers: a mentor at least 5 ranks higher in the same city gives ×1.5, a Training Ground ×2, and
above rank 60 training gives ×0.5.

**Breakthroughs** at ranks 20, 40, 50, 60, 70 and 80 need a **Trial**:

| Rank | Trial |
|---|---|
| 20 | 3 training sessions |
| 40 | Win a spar or duel, or defeat 5 foes |
| 50 | Defeat 10 foes |
| 60 | Defeat an elite, or win a duel |
| 70 | Win 2 duels |
| 80 | 300 renown, or an Archon's blessing |

Each passed Trial adds an **Ascension star** and plays a ceremony.

**Talents:** one point every 3 ranks, spent by personality. Each level gives +8% damage and 3% shorter cooldowns.

**Constellations** come from fateful moments: surviving near death, slaying a king, winning a duel,
defeating an elite, 50 foes, 500 renown, becoming a ruler, Ascension ★3 and ★6.
- C3 and C5: +2 to all talents.
- C6: the Burst is awakened (×1.5 power, impact frames).

**Beyond:**
- **Exaltation:** at rank 70, ★5, C4 a bearer may become an Adeptus, a Harbinger, a Witch or an Abyss Herald.
- **Godhood:** an Exalted at rank 90 and C6 may claim an **empty Archon throne**.
- **Succession:** when an Archon dies, the Gnosis passes to the best successor.

## 7. Battle

- **Moves:** strikes with smears, flurries, dashes with afterimages, teleports, projectiles, beams,
  fields (tornadoes, ice spikes, blooms), meteor and lightning rains, shields, heals and **ultimates**.
- **Reactions:** elements react, and a floating label names the reaction:
  - Vaporize, Melt
  - Overloaded, Superconduct
  - Electro-Charged, Frozen, Burning, Bloom, Aggravate
  - Swirl, Crystallize
- **Duels:** when two powers meet on screen they may duel. A spotlight and a blade ring start it. Then
  come exchanges, barrages with knockback, parries and counters, weaving dodges leaving afterimages,
  and combos. The healthier fighter ends it with a **finisher**.
- **Impact frames** for big hits: hit-stop, a white flash, a black frame with speed lines, an element
  frame, a camera punch and a boom. The ground is left with a **crater**.
- **Ultimate cutscenes** for Archons and above. The ultimate starts with a spotlight and closing rings,
  then a barrage of strikes, a blast, a shockwave and a crater.

## 8. Settings

Open them from **Archive → Chronicle → Settings**, or **Mods → Teyvat Chronicle**.

| Group | What you can change |
|---|---|
| Chronicle | Pacing, start era, custom time scale, catch-up, countdowns, minor events |
| Announcements | Banner (and how long it stays), flash, shake, sound, camera jump, world log, lore pop-ups |
| Eras | Turn each of the 11 eras on or off |
| Rules | Unique holders, destructive effects, wars |
| Combat | Moves, move damage, cooldown speed, reactions, duels (chance, how many at once), sounds, labels, tier auras, dragon designs |
| Cinematics | Impact frames and their intensity, move cutscenes and their spacing, Chronicle cutscenes, spotlight, ceremonies, afterimages, craters |
| Resonance | On/off, training and combat XP, sparring, awakening and its rate, succession, Exaltation, Godhood, legend announcements |
| Interface | Resonance panel, HUD, Teyvat UI size |

## 9. Recipes

- **Epic fights right away:** Archive → Chronicle → click *The Traveler Awakens* → Start here. With
  catch-up, the Seven, the Harbingers and the Shades are already in place.
- **Raise a champion:** mark a Training Ground in a capital, Grant Vision to a young warrior, and watch
  their panel climb. A higher-rank mentor in the same city helps.
- **A world of dragons:** start at the Old World on Lore pacing and turn off the eras after the War of
  Funerary Flame.
- **Pure hero sim:** never press Begin. Visions awaken on their own as people train and fight.
- **Too much spectacle?** Raise "Seconds between move cutscenes", turn off move cutscenes but keep the
  Chronicle ones, or lower the impact-frame intensity.
- **Performance on huge maps:** effects only play near the camera. If it's still heavy, turn off tier
  auras and afterimages first.

## 10. Troubleshooting

- **No Teyvat tab:** NeoModLoader isn't installed, or the folder name is wrong. `mod.json` must sit
  directly in `Mods/TeyvatChronicle/`.
- **The mod doesn't load after editing a file:** check the NML log. A broken `chronicle.custom.json` or
  `moves.custom.json` stops loading. Delete it to go back to the defaults.
- **No Resonance panel:** check *Interface → Resonance panel*.
- **Something looks wrong:** send the NML log and say what you clicked.
